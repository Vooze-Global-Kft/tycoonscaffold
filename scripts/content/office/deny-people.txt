# Heads of state and government, their deputies, cabinet-rank ministers and opposition leaders of
# the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland and South Africa since
# 1900 (complete for heads of government, presidents, vice-presidents and leaders of the main
# opposition; the best-known for the rest), royals, and notorious public figures. Compiled offline.
#
# How it is matched:
#   - a line with ONE word is a surname. It is compared with every word of a text that is
#     capitalised in the middle of a sentence, and with every word of a name field (names.json,
#     a rival's name, a place, a monument), never with lower-case words or a sentence's first word.
#     It matches when the word (without a possessive "'s") is the same ignoring case, or is one edit
#     away (both at least 4 letters), or sounds alike (same first letter, same consonant code and
#     same first vowel sound; both at least 4 letters). An exact match always fails; a near match
#     fails until the sensitivity editor clears it in that name's names.json check record.
#   - a line with SEVERAL words is a full name, used where the surname is a common word
#     ("Theresa May", "John Major", "Gordon Brown"); it matches those words in sequence, any case.
#   - words in an all-caps run are compared exactly only.
# Surnames that are also everyday words appear only as full names, so "a major ribbon" and "Market
# Ward" pass. "#" starts a comment.
