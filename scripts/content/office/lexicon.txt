# lexicon.txt: everyday English words for tier 2 of the satire lint (office-design.md 7.8). A word on this
# list is never near-matched to a real or fictional name where its capital letter says nothing: as the
# first word of a sentence, inside an all-caps run (a headline, a clipping, a picket) or in a Title Case
# line ("Paint the bench" is not "Paine"; "GIVEN" is not a name). Exact matches of surnames are
# checked everywhere regardless. Compiled by the content desk from the lower-case words of the prose
# of the project's design documents (code spans removed, seen at least twice), plus the game's own vocabulary; surnames of
# deny-people.txt and deny-fiction.txt are removed. Several words a line, split at commas;
# "#" starts a comment. A writer who meets a false near match on an everyday word asks the content desk to add the word here.
