# deny-innuendo.txt: 7.4's "no innuendo" rule (no vegetable, fruit or object is ever soft, hard, long,
# big or "at length"), in three groups:
#   - "always": words with no plain use in this game's voice; tier 3, any case, wherever they appear.
#   - "size words" and "objects": a size word within three words of an object in the same sentence is
#     an error ("a big marrow", "the marrow is very long"); both in one text but further apart is a
#     warning for a reader. A size word alone passes ("as long as", "a big day", "hard pencil",
#     "a new member", "the tool shed"): grammar and committee words are not innuendo.
# Matching: the syntax of deny-phrases.txt (whole words, any case; several entries a line split at
# commas; "word*" any ending). allow.json lets fixed names through ("The Very Long Bridge").
# What only a reader catches: innuendo by context ("the finest rod in town", "very impressive",
# "the judges could not stop looking"); office-style.md lists the words to avoid.

knockers, bulging, bulge, bulges, throbbing, moist, length of his, length of her
