# deny-phrases.txt: TIER 1 of the satire lint (docs/design/office-design.md 7.8). Always fails.
# Real slogans, catchphrases and stock lines of politics in the English-speaking world (US, UK,
# Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland, South Africa) and the best-known ones beyond, compiled
# offline from general knowledge. Owned by the content desk; the sensitivity editor extends it.
#
# How it is matched (same syntax in every deny-*.txt except deny-people and deny-fiction):
#   - one phrase per line; "#" starts a comment line; blank lines are ignored;
#   - any case: the text and the phrase are both lower-cased, accents removed, every run of
#     characters other than letters, digits and apostrophes becomes one space (so a hyphen is a
#     space: "goose-step" matches "goose step"), and the phrase must match whole words;
#   - "word*" matches the word with any ending ("trump*": trumpet, trumped);
#   - a lone "*" stands for one to four words ("make * great again");
#   - "{a,b,c}" is one of the alternatives ("drain the {swamp,marsh,bog}");
#   - a line starting "=" is matched case-sensitively and never at the start of a sentence (for
#     words that are harmless in lower case: "=Turkey" catches the country, not the bird).
#   - a line starting "==" is matched exactly as written, in any position, all-caps runs included.
# Runs of all-caps words are lower-cased before matching, so "=" entries miss them.
# The templates section holds the SHAPES of famous slogans with any words in the gaps ("make * grate
# again", "tough on *, tough on the causes of *"): a parody with one word swapped is still the slogan.
# allow.json lists the exact phrases that pass although they contain a match.
