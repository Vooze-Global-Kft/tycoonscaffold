Lantern Row: Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the mobile game Lantern Row ("the game") handles, why, and what you can do about it. The game is published by [DEVELOPER NAME: set store.developerName] ("we").

You can play Lantern Row without creating an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and the game has no chat.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved on your device. They are included in your device's own backup (iCloud or a computer backup on iPhone, Google's backup on Android) if you have backups turned on, so a new or restored device can continue your game. We do not receive a copy of your save.

Information handled by the services built into the game

The game includes software from other companies. They receive the information below directly from your device and handle it under their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob (advertising: rewarded videos you choose to watch). AdMob receives device identifiers such as the advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), and information about the ads shown and tapped. It uses them to show ads, to limit how often you see the same ad and to prevent fraud; with your permission it also uses them to show ads based on your interests. Policy: policies.google.com/privacy; how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config). Firebase receives an app-instance identifier, device and system information (model, operating system version, language, country), what happens in the game (for example sessions, levels reached, ads watched, purchases made) and crash reports. We use this to see how the game is played, to fix errors and to tune the game. Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • RevenueCat (in-app purchases). Purchases are paid through your Apple or Google account; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and your purchase history in this game, so that purchases can be confirmed and restored. Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Apple Game Center (leaderboards, on iPhone). If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your scores to Apple so they can appear on the leaderboards under your Game Center name. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy. You can sign out of Game Center in your device's Settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your own device (for example when your offline earnings are full). They are created on the device: no notification data is sent to us and we do not use a push service. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your device's settings at any time.

Your choices

  • Ad consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), the game asks for your choice before showing personalised ads. You can change it at any time under "Privacy choices" in the game's settings.
  • Tracking on iPhone. The game asks for permission before using your device's advertising identifier. You can change this in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Advertising ID on Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings, Google, Ads.
  • Deleting your data. Deleting the game removes your save from the device. To ask for the deletion of information held by the services above, or to ask what is held, write to us at [CONTACT EMAIL: set store.contactEmail] and tell us which game and which device you use; we will pass the request on to the services that can identify the data.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. We do not sell personal information.

Children

Lantern Row is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, write to us at [CONTACT EMAIL: set store.contactEmail] and we will have it deleted.

How long information is kept

Analytics and crash information is kept by the services above for the periods set in their own policies (Firebase Analytics data for up to 14 months). Purchase records are kept as long as they are needed to restore your purchases and to meet legal obligations.
