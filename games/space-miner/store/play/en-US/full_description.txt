Start with a single hand drill on a lonely asteroid and grow a mining company that spans the galaxy.

• Build seven kinds of machines, from rovers to mirrors around a star
• Give every machine an AI pilot so it runs without you
• Collect a small crew of specialists and level them up
• A free supply pod drops every few hours, and daily missions pay in Dark Matter
• Earn credits while the app is closed
• Jump to a new galaxy to earn Warp Cores and grow faster every run
• Keep one step ahead of Director Rook and his rock monopoly

Asteroid Haul is a calm space incremental game for short breaks.
