Turn Nonna's tiny slice stand into the biggest pizza empire in the universe!

• Tap to bake, then hire chefs to run your kitchens while you're away
• Unlock 10 businesses, from food trucks to a pizza planet
• Earn money even when the app is closed
• Franchise your empire to earn Michelin Stars and grow faster every run
• Outsmart your rival, Big Cheese Carl

Pizza Empire is a relaxing idle tycoon game you can play in short bursts all day.
