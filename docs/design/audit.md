# Second-line audit: engine, store and pizza-empire data

Adversarial pass over `src/engine`, `src/state/store.ts` and `games/pizza-empire/game.json`
(generated by `scripts/content/build-pizza.py`), after the first review (`engine-review.md`) and
the design critique (`critique-fun.md`). Every exploit below has a test that failed before its fix
(`src/engine/audit.test.ts`, `src/engine/firstSession.test.ts`).

## 1. Exploits found and fixed

| # | What a player could do | Proof (test) | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Farm the daily gift with the clock**: claim, set the clock back a day, claim, forward again, claim. `canClaimDaily` used `!==` on the day key. | `the daily gift cannot be farmed with the clock` | `canClaimDaily`: only a day LATER than the last claim. |
| 2 | **Re-roll a chest**: every roll used the shared `state.rng`, so claiming the free random card (or opening another chest kind) first, or reloading after a different action, changed what a chest held. | `a chest cannot be re-rolled` | Each chest id has its own stream: `chests.seed` (fixed at creation) x the id x `chests.opened[id]`. The Nth chest of a kind always opens the same way. Only the shared bad-luck counters link chests, and they only raise a slot. |
| 3 | **Hostile save**: `missions.slots` with `null`, NaN targets, `completed: 'x'`, deal `purchases: -5` (7 purchases left of 2), a timed deal with `startedAt: 'x'` (permanent), `venuesEver: 1e300` (copies x2e299), duplicate daily-shop offers. Crashes and free stuff. | `hostile or broken saves` (3 tests) | `reconcileState` checks every mission slot, completion count, deal state (purchases clamped to the limit, a broken start time counts as long ago), daily-shop ids (deduplicated, known ids only), opened-chest counts, and caps `venuesEver` at the number of venues. `missionViews`/`refreshMissions` skip a null slot. |
| 4 | **Crash on odd configs**: a chest whose only slot kind is cards, in a game with no cards, threw in `rollCard`. A free or ad chest pointing at a missing chest showed "ready" but claimed nothing. | `odd configs` (4 tests) | A chest with no possible slot pays gems; `freeChestStatus` / `adChestStatus` are null for a missing chest. |
| 5 | **Paid chests where not allowed**: the store sold a deal or a paid daily offer containing a chest without the region check. | `chests for sale only where allowed` | New `M.offerAllowed(cfg, region, rewards, paid)`; the store calls it before every deal and daily-offer purchase. |
| 6 | **Farm offline earnings with the clock**: forward 4 h (paid 4 h), back to the real time (the store reset `lastSavedAt` to now), forward again (paid again). The store no longer pre-clamps `lastSavedAt` in `resume`. |
| 7 | **Money taken for nothing**: `purchase(product)` for a product of kind `deal` charged and granted nothing (deal products grant through `buyDeal`). A throwing `applyProduct` after payment lost the purchase silently. | store read (section 6) | `purchase` routes deal products to `buyDeal`; `applyProduct` is wrapped and a failure is reported. |

Checked and found safe (tests in `audit.test.ts`): claiming a mission, the free chest or the free
daily item twice at the same millisecond; buying a deal past its limit or after it expired; gems
going negative through deals, time warps or chests; levelling a card with too few copies; opening
a chest that is not owned, fractional or negative chest counts from a save; the ad-chest limit,
the daily shop and the mission limit under clock moves (forward gives tomorrow's early, never
extra); counters near 1e308 stay finite; a config with no cards, no chests and one mission
template runs. Moving the clock forward claims the next free chest earlier, but storage is capped
and the anchor moves with it, so nothing is gained over time (declined: the earlier review's
`clampMetaClock` would let a forward-then-back toggle print chests).

## 2. The drop-rate screen: exact-odds verdict

`M.chestOddsFacts(cfg, chestId, state | null)` computes the odds exactly from the config and the
rules `openChest` follows, and is tested against 60,000 real opens (within 0.5 points) with a
guarantee, and with bad-luck protection:

- `slotOdds`, `cardsPerChest` (the guarantee's extra card included);
- `perCard`: the true share of each rarity among the cards a chest gives (sums to 100). With a
  guarantee this differs from the base weights (Chef's Crate: Common 54.4 %, not 60 %), and the
  old screen (`chestCardOdds`) would have overstated commons;
- `perChest[r].atLeastPercent`: chests with at least one card of rarity r or better;
- `guaranteed`, `epicWithin`, `legendaryWithin`, `copiesMultiplier`;
- `longRun`: the same with protection, over many chests of this kind (exact renewal-reward over the
  protection counters, not a simulation), e.g. Pizza Box: Epic or better 14.5 % per chest without
  protection, 19.2 % in the long run; Legendary 2.5 % -> 3.9 %;
- `nextChest`: this player's next chest from their counters (100 % of the chests with cards when
  protection is due);
- `lines`: every sentence of the screen, built from those numbers ("<0.1 %" for tiny chances,
  certainties exactly "100 %").

Verdict: with this function the screen is exactly true. `chestCardOdds` alone is not when the chest
has a guarantee; screens should use `chestOddsFacts`. Its shape is final.

## 3. Engine gaps closed (critique-fun.md)

- Missions: no "rush" mission before any transport automation hire, no "chests" mission before the
  player has ever owned a chest.
- Daily gift: counts days CLAIMED (a missed day never resets it) and the table cycles, so 7 entries
  are a weekly calendar: 115 gems a week, about 16 a day, which brings free gems to the research's
  45 a day (section 5). `E.dailyReward` now also returns `day` (1-based position in the table) and
  the new `E.dailyCalendar` returns the row to draw, so the row always equals what a claim pays.
- Store: `refreshMissions` gets `now`; no interstitial after closing a chest; the three refreshes
  return the same object when nothing changed (tested), so the 2 s housekeeping does not re-render.
- First session: `src/engine/firstSession.test.ts` plays a new game following the hand one action a
  second. The lessons come in the script's order: tap the oven, ring the bell, buy the oven, hire
  the Pizzaiolo (at 80 s), hire the Maitre d' (108 s), the rush, the Antipasti Counter, the Prep
  Cook, the first recipe, then missions and chests; no hint ever points at something that cannot be
  done. Fixes in `nextHint`: missions and chests are pointed at only once the first venue is
  automated; no "buy" while the next hire is the goal (a station at its first milestone, or a hire
  within 60 s of income, or the transport hire once a station is automated); before the transport
  hire only production is bought; a player is a "veteran" only once every owned station is hired.
  `guideHint` adds the early lessons (rush, next station, first upgrade) ahead of plain buys.

## 7. The reset loop

`economy.prestige`: threshold 3e15, scale 20 (was 1), bonusPerPoint 0.02. The first Chef Star
exists at lifetime 7.5e12 (1 h 21 for the non-stop bot); a reset that doubles income needs 50
stars (lifetime 1.9e16, 2 h 35 for the bot; for the 35-minutes-a-day player, the end of day 1). Before:
the first reset (bot, 3 h 18) gave 3 stars, +6 %.

Bot with `--prestige` (now: reset when it at least doubles the prestige multiplier), 48 h:
runs of 2 h 35, 1 h 50, 1 h 13, 48 min, 34 min, 30 min, 25 min, 23 min, 18 min, 16 min, then about
15 min (the bot's floor) for resets 11 to 21, then longer again: 19, 20, 23, 24, 36 min, 1 h, 1 h 41,
3 h, 3 h, 5 h 41, 10 h 51 (32 resets, prestige multiplier x9.7e11, lifetime 3.9e46, all finite and
shown as letter suffixes by format.ts up to 1.8e308).

Verdict: each run is clearly faster than the last for the first ten, and the decision is real
(the reset screen shows the multiplier after; waiting longer pays more stars but costs time). The
middle phase is degenerate for a non-stop player: while the venue ladder is still ahead, a x2
multiplier opens later venues sooner and a run's earnings grow much faster than x4, so resets
cascade to the 15-minute floor; once a run reaches the last venue, growth comes only from levels,
milestones and the late upgrades, and the loop settles into resets that take hours and then days.
For the modelled 35-minutes-a-day player that is 12 resets in week one, then 3 more in week two and
one every 10 to 15 days by day 90. bonusPerPoint and threshold only scale this; its shape comes from
points = scale x sqrt(lifetime / threshold) and a linear bonus, which data cannot change (see
"Remaining risks").

## 8. How long the content lasts

Nothing ever runs out of things to buy: station levels and `milestoneRepeat` (x2 every 100 levels)
repeat forever. What runs out: the 8th and last venue opens at 19 h 48 of non-stop play without
resets, at day 7 for the modelled player; the old upgrades end at 5e17 (bought at 4 h 11). Data
extension made: 12 more "all" x2 upgrades from 5e25 to 5e69, x10,000 apart, past the last venue's
cost, so every late run has a next goal for many more resets (bot: still buying them at 48 h).
Further data-only levers: more of that ladder (up to 1e300), and more venues (each needs a name,
place and icon in `pizza-sim-content.json`; costs continue at UNLOCK_STEP x80 and scale x40, but the
screens' per-venue art and palettes would need entries too).


## 9. Store re-read (G)

Every action handles a null engine result; `update` keeps the ads flags in sync and `reset` now does
too; saved after everything paid for or earned for good (claimMission, buyDailyOffer, claimDaily,
levelUpCard, gemTimeWarp, story gems, rewarded boost and rush reset, offline collect, deals,
purchases, chests); `buy`, `hire` and `startBusiness` ignore unknown ids instead of throwing;

`purchase()` of a deal product goes through `buyDeal`.

## Remaining risks

- Prestige shape (section 7): an optional `economy.prestige.exponent` (default 0.5) in the schema,
  used by `totalPrestigePoints` as `scale * (lifetime / threshold) ^ exponent`, would let a game
  damp the cascade (1/3 is the usual choice). Schema change, not made: `src/config` is not mine.
- Cards are all owned in about a week for an engaged free player (section 5).
- `chestCardOdds` stays the base roll; any screen that shows it alone for a chest with a guarantee
  overstates commons. Use `chestOddsFacts`.
- The daily-gift row in `src/ui/meta/shop/DailyGift.tsx` assumes the last gift repeats past the end of
  the table; the table now cycles. It shows today's amount correctly (from `dailyReward`), but the
  row after day 7 is wrong until it uses `E.dailyCalendar`.
- `formatNumber` shows 999,995 as "999.99K" rather than "1.00M" (cosmetic; format.ts is shared).
