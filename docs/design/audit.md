# Second-line audit: engine, store and pizza-empire data

Adversarial pass over `src/engine`, `src/state/store.ts` and `games/pizza-empire/game.json`
(generated by `scripts/content/build-pizza.py`), after the first review (`engine-review.md`) and
the design critique (`critique-fun.md`). Every exploit below has a test that failed before its fix
(`src/engine/audit.test.ts`, `src/engine/firstSession.test.ts`).

## 1. Exploits found and fixed

Checked and found safe (tests in `audit.test.ts`): claiming a mission, the free chest or the free

going negative through deals, time warps or chests; levelling a card with too few copies; opening

extra); counters near 1e308 stay finite; a config with no cards, no chests and one mission

and the anchor moves with it, so nothing is gained over time (declined: the earlier review's
`clampMetaClock` would let a forward-then-back toggle print chests).

## 2. The drop-rate screen: exact-odds verdict

`M.chestOddsFacts(cfg, chestId, state | null)` computes the odds exactly from the config and the
rules `openChest` follows, and is tested against 60,000 real opens (within 0.5 points) with a
guarantee, and with bad-luck protection:

- `slotOdds`, `cardsPerChest` (the guarantee's extra card included);
- `perCard`: the true share of each rarity among the cards a chest gives (sums to 100). With a
  guarantee this differs from the base weights (Chef's Crate: Common 54.4 %, not 60 %), and the
  old screen (`chestCardOdds`) would have overstated commons;
- `perChest[r].atLeastPercent`: chests with at least one card of rarity r or better;
- `guaranteed`, `epicWithin`, `legendaryWithin`, `copiesMultiplier`;
- `longRun`: the same with protection, over many chests of this kind (exact renewal-reward over the
  protection counters, not a simulation), e.g. Pizza Box: Epic or better 14.5 % per chest without
  protection, 19.2 % in the long run; Legendary 2.5 % -> 3.9 %;
- `nextChest`: this player's next chest from their counters (100 % of the chests with cards when
  protection is due);
- `lines`: every sentence of the screen, built from those numbers ("<0.1 %" for tiny chances,
  certainties exactly "100 %").

Verdict: with this function the screen is exactly true. `chestCardOdds` alone is not when the chest
has a guarantee; screens should use `chestOddsFacts`. Its shape is final.

## 3. Engine gaps closed (critique-fun.md)

- Missions: no "rush" mission before any transport automation hire, no "chests" mission before the
  player has ever owned a chest.
- Daily gift: counts days CLAIMED (a missed day never resets it) and the table cycles, so 7 entries
  are a weekly calendar: 115 gems a week, about 16 a day, which brings free gems to the research's
  45 a day (section 5). `E.dailyReward` now also returns `day` (1-based position in the table) and
  the new `E.dailyCalendar` returns the row to draw, so the row always equals what a claim pays.
- Store: `refreshMissions` gets `now`; no interstitial after closing a chest; the three refreshes
  return the same object when nothing changed (tested), so the 2 s housekeeping does not re-render.
- First session: `src/engine/firstSession.test.ts` plays a new game following the hand one action a
  second. The lessons come in the script's order: tap the oven, ring the bell, buy the oven, hire
  the Pizzaiolo (at 80 s), hire the Maitre d' (108 s), the rush, the Antipasti Counter, the Prep
  Cook, the first recipe, then missions and chests; no hint ever points at something that cannot be
  done. Fixes in `nextHint`: missions and chests are pointed at only once the first venue is
  automated; no "buy" while the next hire is the goal (a station at its first milestone, or a hire
  within 60 s of income, or the transport hire once a station is automated); before the transport
  hire only production is bought; a player is a "veteran" only once every owned station is hired.
  `guideHint` adds the early lessons (rush, next station, first upgrade) ahead of plain buys.
