## 2. The rules

Pure TypeScript in `src/engine/salvage/`, deterministic, one `advance` for a tick, a jump and time away. The order below is the order of building. 2.1 to 2.4 are the engine step that opens the stage (ruling 10.4; E36(a)): built, benched and measured before any later rule, and nothing in them depends on the look.

### 2.1 The engine step: crew trips grouped out of sight (ruling 10.4, D87; as ruled in G1 to G3; as built)

The engine step is built and tested (label `engine2-group`; its account `notes/engine2-group.md`, status 03:40; files `src/engine/salvage/{group.ts (new), crew.ts, truck.ts, round.ts, hand.ts, tick.ts, clock.ts, machines.ts, things.ts, scales.ts, types.ts, save.ts}`, tests `group.test.ts`, `tickjump.test.ts`, `awaybench.test.ts`). This section is the rule as built, in the builder's sentences, under the lead's G1 to G3. Its bench meets the stage's gate of 250 ms (ruling 13.9; 2.1.3). 2.2 to 2.4 are the engine step's next part and are not yet built: they are written to the lead's H1, I1 to I6 and D1.

**2.1.1 The rule.** From rung `group.fromRung` (3), crew work that STARTS with no boss within sight is a RUN: its legs are planned at that departure, all it will take is claimed then, and it has ONE dated end (`CrewMember.run`, `CrewRun {t0, end, start, k, legS, dest?, loads?, cuts?}`). "Within sight": a fresh boss within `reach.sight` (10 m) of the run's start point, its sources or its destination (a place counts by its footprint; `group.ts bossSees`). The decision is made when the run starts, kept in the state and never recomputed. Away no boss is fresh, so all work is grouped; live, the same where he is not; a tick, a jump and time away call one function. Below `fromRung` nothing changes (stage 1's legs). L16: the hour the owner played stays as measured; the bots try grouping from rung 1 the next time the first hour is measured anyway, and the lead decides.

**What is promised** (G1): (a) a tick equals a jump, exactly, with grouping on: for the SAME presence state (the same presence samples, or none), ticking, one jump and many jumps give the same state; (b) the same function runs present and away. It is NOT promised that a yard with the boss present but out of sight equals the same yard with the boss away: the first yard's rules make them differ on purpose, and these differences are the rule (B3):
1. While the boss is in the yard, yard hands carry pieces only (P11 of the first yard); whole things that need no cutting (tier 0) are cleared from the tipping floor only when he is away or the floor is over half full (E35, `crew.ts FLOOR_CLEAR_SHARE`).
2. The foreman's boost runs only live (`clock.ts boostOn`); the hand stripper works only with the boss (`machines.ts`) and is never grouped.
3. Hold's release (2.2) fires only when the boss is not fresh.
4. The away pass runs the checks (rung, rivals, deal offer, goals) at most every 60 s of yard time and at its end (`clock.ts CHECK_EVERY_S`; it was 5 s); live play checks at every instant. On the bench's five yards and the bot hour this changed no state at all.
A tagged thing is not a difference: its owner is decided by sight, present and away alike (2.12, E4, B15). The built test "present equals away" (`group.test.ts`) runs on a content where the presence rules above cannot apply (no cable and no loose things in any source); it may stay as a test of that content, and promises nothing beyond G1.

**2.1.2 What is grouped** (as built).
- **Yard hand** (`group.handRun_s`, 30 s, tune): his first trip is chosen exactly as in stage 1 (S12's order, P11, E35, E4). More trips OF THE SAME KIND, from the same source to the same destination, are added by the same in-place choice while the run (departure to the last drop) stays within `handRun_s`; the first trip always goes. The kinds: a feed from one pile to one machine (no more than the hopper's room at departure); pieces from one pile (a circle, the drop-off's pile, the floor, the pieces beside one thing); one out tray; loose things at one place; E35's whole floor trip to one destination; E4's whole thing (only while no cutter is hired, or at the drop-off while every place holds a thing nobody has claimed). Each trip keeps its own two leg ends (walk + handle, walk back + handle, as a leg; `RunLoad {claims, spot, grade, pickAt, dropAt}`). At the run's end every trip lands in order: picked up, then weighed in as its own weigh-in (each grade line rounded once, the ticket dated at that trip's drop with that day's prices) or fed (what the hopper cannot take is weighed in at once). A run that claims an out tray restarts a machine stopped by that full tray at once (the claimed grams are his from the departure).
- **Cutter** (`group.cutRun_s`, 120 s, tune): his first thing as in stage 1 (the drop-off first, then the floor, best score first); more things at the same place in the same score order while the run stays within `cutRun_s` (the first always; `RunCut {thing, walkEnd, cutEnd}`). Each is walked to and cut whole; at the end each is cut in order, its pieces on its place's pile.
- **Driver**: the seven phases of a trip (walk, out, hook, back, weigh, tip, park) are planned at departure with the durations stage 1 gives them (`truck.trip.plan`, `TripPlan {t0, ends[7], x, y}`), one dated end at the park. At the end the phases are replayed at their planned instants: the hook paid (it was earmarked since departure) and the empty skip set down dated at the hook's instant (if it would already be full, it is full at the landing), the bridge ticket dated at the weigh's end, the load tipped, the truck parked. Not grouped: the driver taking over a loaded truck the boss left (R2), rare.
- **Merchant**: a round that leaves with no boss within sight of his stand (the merchant's place or the bays) is away max(`round_s`, `group.roundAway_s` 120 s, tune 60 to 300).
- **Rate machines** (the first yard's stripper and bench; 2.7 to 2.8's plates when they exist): a piece started with no boss within sight of the machine takes every hopper piece of its grade into one segment (credited by the cumulative rule over the summed grams; `MachineWork.n` counts the pieces). A machine's grouped segment is never opened (nothing a person does touches it). A back-field plate drawing from a bay (2.8.1) runs in segments that end only at the events of 2.8.1 (L8). Measured: on the bench it changed nothing (hoppers rarely hold two pieces of a grade at a start).
- **The magnet crane** (2.7, when built): moves started out of sight are one run of moves (`WorksState.move`, 2.18).
- Not grouped: the drop-off, skips filling, purchases arriving, the crucible, the day; in stage 2 also visitors, the van, lots and the crate (each one event already).

**The run limits** (measured; six hours away on five played minute-60 yards, seeds 257, 1, 5, 9, 12, sales against grouping off): the draft's one `maxRun_s` of 300 s lost 9.8 % of sales (−8.1 to −10.9 % a yard); hands 30 s with the cutter 120 s lost 0.1 % (−0.8 to +1.3 % a yard; each yard varies about ± 2 % with any change of timing). Long hand runs lose because the tipping floor's room binds (about 2,900 of 4,000 kg in use all six hours; the truck waits for room or skips 46 % of the time): a leg-by-leg hand re-chooses by S12's whole order at every departure and takes the cutter's newest pieces, while a long run holds its claims and its order. A content `group.maxRun_s`, if given, caps both limits.

**Opening a run** (G3; built, and its tests show it exact, so it is kept). At every live instant and at every presence sample (`hand.ts bossStep`), a run the boss can see (its start, a source or its destination within `reach.sight` of him) is OPENED at that instant t: what its plan finished by t lands at t in order, exactly as at its end (each trip picked and delivered, each ticket dated at its own drop; each thing cut whole; each phase replayed at its instant); the leg under way goes on as ONE ORDINARY LEG with its planned times (a hand walking to a trip: a walk-to leg with its claims; walking back: the trip is picked now and he walks back; a cutter cutting: a work leg, whole, as decided; walking to a thing: a walk-to leg whose end decides by stage 1's sight rule; the truck: the phase under way); the rest of the plan is released (claims cleared). The merchant's long round ends at max(t, its start + `round_s`). No action opens a run: nothing a run holds can be taken by anyone else (claims), and presence is the only opener.

**What the screen shows.** While a run holds a member, his `phase`, `from`, `to`, `t0`, `t1`, `carries` (a cutter's `cut`) are set to the leg of his plan under way at the end of every advance (tick, jump and away alike), a pure function of plan and clock, so the frame draws him walking and cutting as before; the truck's phase, `t0`, `endsAt` and position likewise. The queue reads the run's end (`run.end`, `trip.plan.ends[6]`), never these fields. Two things the frame cannot see from them (`requests.md`, `[engine2-group]` to the screens): a yard hand walking back on a run has `load` empty (his pieces stay claimed until the run lands), so the barrow is drawn loaded when `run && phase === 'walkBack'` (grade in `carries`); the truck on a planned trip has `skip` null until it lands, so a full skip is drawn from phase `back` on.

**What is not exact against stage 1's legs, and is the rule** (not a tolerance): (1) a run's work becomes visible to the rest of the yard at its end (or its opening), not at each trip's own drop: the merchant, the crucible and the floor's room see it up to a run later; (2) which trips a run makes is planned at its departure from what waits then (pieces the cutter drops during the run, a tray that fills during a floor run, a level or the foreman's boost begun during a run, wait for the next run); (3) so the economy moves where the floor's room binds, which is why the hand's limit is 30 s; (4) a skip set down by a grouped hook that would already be full when the trip lands is full at the landing; (5) a feed trip whose hopper is fuller at landing than planned weighs the rest in at once.

**The bound on what grouping may change** (G2): with grouping switched off in the content (`group.fromRung` above the top rung) and on, the bots' three days at the twice-a-day habit give supplied tonnes and cash within 2 % of each other at day 1 and at day 3 on at least 13 of 15 seeds, and no crew trip is lost (mass delivered by crew over a day equal within one run). Below `group.fromRung` nothing changes at all. As built: with grouping off, the whole state after a bot hour, a three-hour away pass and 60 s of ticks is bit-identical to the stage-1 engine (sha1 of the JSON); the bots' first-hour tables (15 seeds, 99 runs) reproduce exactly (rung 3 at 57:08, 14 of 15); the three days have not yet been compared with grouping off: that is the first of Phase A's measurements still owed (9.1).

### 2.2 Hold never stops the yard (E40, ruling 10.5, D88; H1)

At a driver's departure check (2.14 of the first yard, step 5), when the boss is not fresh, a driver is idle with a full skip waiting, cash above the earmarks is below that skip's price, and the merchant waits with nothing collectable, the held bay worth most at today's price leaves Hold and the round loads it at once: one bay a check. Event `holdReleased {grade}`; the away card's line "Sold from hold to keep the yard running: {grade}" is printed only when a bay was sold from hold (H1). Grams set aside by Sell are already sold and are not touched; a bay's reserve for a station (2.8.1) is not held and is not released.

Worked example (`yard-bots.md` C5, seed 1): at the end of sitting 2 every one of 14 bays was held and cash was §524; the next 11.75 h away paid §0. ### 2.3 Rated income, I, from assets (rulings 2.1, 10.6, 12; I1 to I6; D27)

**2.3.1 The measure** (fixed in form). `ratedIncome(c, s)`, § an hour, NET (I1, L2): what the yard keeps in an hour with its present assets, no boss, at usual prices (p̄): lot payments less what the driver pays for loads. It is the steady state of 2.1's grouped engine in closed form over the same rate functions:
1. **Feed**, kg/s by thing (I3): the drop-off's arrivals over its period, and the partners' loads by I2: **loads an hour = min(Σ over open partners of 3,600 × L_p ÷ `fill_s`_p, 3,600 × trucks ÷ t̄_trip)**, where t̄_trip is the planned round trip weighted by the mix of partners served, the same planned time the driver's run uses (2.1.2: walk, out, hook, back, weigh, tip, park), and the trucks' loads are given to partners by net margin a load, best first (E1, I2). Each load brings its things and its reusable batch.
2. **Steps**, each a capacity over the share of feed mass that needs it: cutting (the cutters' power over the mix's hardness, plus the shear); carrying (yard hands, plus the magnet crane on ferrous within its reach); each plate's rate on what it takes.
3. **R_bind** = min(Σ feed kg/s, min over steps of capacity ÷ that share) (B12): capacity of hands, cutters and machines beyond the feed adds nothing to I (I3).
4. **m̄** = the value at p̄ of what a kilogram of feed becomes with the plates owned, less the label price of bought feed a kilogram, less reagents a kilogram (E3). I never reads a switch the player can flip (I4, E2, T26; it replaces ruling 10.6's "lowest margin"): not routes, recipes, Hold or "Take from bays" as set. It assigns each flow of material to the owned plates' capacity by margin a kilogram, best first, in one greedy pass, and sells the rest as weighed.
5. **I** = I₀ + the Yard sale's premium + the van's premium, where I₀ = 3,600 × R_bind × m̄, the Yard sale's premium is 0.6 of what the rack's ceiling lets it pay an hour (min(the reusable flow's gate value, `reuse.shareI` × max(I₀, `reuse.floorI`)); the other 0.4 is the scrap value already in m̄) and the van's is a third of what it pays an hour (E3). Both read I₀, never I, so nothing is circular.

It is 0 before the first yard hand. The built `ratedOf` counts the whole truck once per partner: it keeps its form for the capacity ceilings only, and is not I (I2). The built `incomeRate` (a moving average of sales) stays for what reads it today; nothing new reads it (I6). **Who writes what** (E12): engine2 writes `ratedIncome` in `rate.ts` with the first yard's terms (Phase A); rules-works writes the plates' terms (each plate's capacity and margin a kilogram, from 2.5.3's data) and asks engine2 for their call in `rate.ts` (a line in `requests.md`).

**2.3.2 Read at each read; frozen only where stored** (B11). A card's numbers (a plate's terms, "Save up", a loan's terms, a pack's quote before Buy) are computed from the state at each read: I moves only when assets do. An action that acts on a number the player was shown carries it and is refused when it differs (`signFinance(plate, shown)` with `bankTerms`, as `buy` with `priceUpdated`). Frozen are only the values stored: a pending quote (`iap.pending`), a lot's mass (fixed on arrival), an agreement's `I`, and the away bonus offer (`ads.awayOffer {amount, I}`).

**2.3.3 Gate** (fixed in form; I5, E3). The value I is held against is measured by the bots with usual prices every day (`board.a` 0 for the gate runs): G = (net cash gained + what the bank took + the value at p̄ of unsold stock at the end − at the start) ÷ hours the yard ran. I is within 0.9 to 1.1 of G on 13 of 15 seeds: in Phase A at rung 3's entry and at the end of three days of the first yard's content (I5); after Phase B also at the last purchase of rung 3 and at the entry and last purchase of rungs 4 and 5 (ruling 10.6, E12). The table is printed; a miss names which term is off and by how much.

**2.3.4 The figure to check first** (L2). The design's §54,500 an hour at rung 3's entry is the Night watch's worth (C9: §106k for two hours), which is sales. The bench's six hours (§444,734 of sales, 232 loads) give about §74,000 of sales an hour; at label prices of §958 and §1,842 the loads cost roughly §250k to §330k, so net I may be §20k to §30k an hour (estimate). Every hour-priced number follows I (packs, the bank's limit, ads, lots, the Yard sale's ceiling): the bots measure I before any of them is tuned, and the worked examples are recomputed when they have. Until then the worked examples of this document keep the ruling's figure (§54,500) so that they can be checked against it (L17).

### 2.14 Plant finance (ruling 3 as corrected by ruling 11; E37, E41; D38, D104)

**2.14.1 The state.** `SalvageState.bank = {agreement: Agreement | null, letterSeen: boolean, done: {plate, T, signedAt, repaidAt}[]}`; `Agreement = {plate, P, F, c, T, taken, signedAt, I}` (§ in hundredths; `taken` rises from 0 to T).

**2.14.2 What may be financed** (ruling 11.3): a plate with `financeable` true (2.5.3), from rung 3, priced P ≥ `bank.minPrice_h` (0.5) h × I. Nothing else: no tool, carrier, vehicle, crew, level, skip, magnet crane, analyser or look. If no rung-3 plate meets the floor, the floor falls (L1, 1.3).

**2.14.3 The terms**, computed from the state at each read (B11; I moves only when assets do): F_max = min(`bank.maxF_h` 2, `bank.maxF_tau` 0.15 × τ_r) × I; F = min(`bank.maxShare` 0.8 × P, F_max) (the card offers this one figure, D38); deposit d = P − F; charge = roundMoney(`bank.charge` 0.08 × F); T = F + charge. `signFinance(plate, shown)` carries the terms the player was shown and refuses, in order: `bankOne` (an agreement is open: "One loan at a time. **2.14.4 Repayment** (ruling 11.1, fixed; B2). Every **lot payment** of gross x goes through one function in `bank.ts`, `payLot(x: Ctx, gross, t): {y, z}`, called at the payment's site BEFORE its ticket prints (the ticket carries `bank: {y}`): it applies `bankSplit(gross, agreements)` (a list, oldest first, never more than `bank.share` of the payment in all; stage 2 passes at most one), y = min(roundMoney(`bank.share` 0.2 × gross), T − taken); `taken` += y; event `bankTaken {y, taken, T}`; then it calls the one `earn(x, z, t)` (`clock.ts`) with the player's part z = gross − y.
