# Stage 2's specification: the lead's rulings on its writers' points (26 November, 03:05)

`docs/design/yard-stage2.md` section 11.2 lists seventeen points for the lead (L1 to L17). The
rulings below outrank the document until its one revision has written them in. "Accepted" means:
as the document has it.

L1. THE FIRST LOAN. Accepted that the baler is bought with cash (its payback price is under the
    bank's floor). The lender's letter comes with the first plate that is dearer than the cash in
    hand and can be financed; the bots' prices decide which (the shear or the depollution bay are
    expected). The aim: the first "Bank loan" is offered within the first two sittings of rung 3
    on at least 12 seeds of 15. If no rung-3 plate carries it, the bank's floor (`bank.minPrice_h`)
    falls; the letter does not wait for rung 4.
L2. RATED INCOME IS NET. I is what the yard keeps: sales less what the driver pays for loads, an
    hour, with present assets, no boss, at usual prices. Its gate is against the away card's NET
    an hour run. Every hour-priced number follows I (packs, the bank's limit, ads, lots, the yard
    sale's ceiling); the examples are recomputed when the bots have measured I.
L3. ADS. The away bonus pays up to 15 minutes of I; each of three spot buyers up to 4 minutes: 27
    minutes a day at most, under 4 % of a rung-3 day (29.5 minutes).
L4. THE SPOT BUYER AND THE BANK. Ruling 9.9's first sentence goes (under ruling 11 every sale
    repays alike). What stays: an ad's reward is always its own line, never part of a payment the
    bank shares in, and no ad button stands beside a card or ticket that shows a debt.
L5. "ABOUT FOUR HOURS" in ruling 11.5 is hours PLAYED; a yard left alone may take about seven. Both
    are measured and printed; neither is a gate beyond "the first loan is repaid before rung 4 on
    every seed at the twice-a-day habit".
L6. Accepted: the content check sets lead's share of rung 5's threshold to what the battery supply
    reaches inside rung 5's span.
L7. Accepted: the design's 1,000 t with range 8 and 10,000 t with range 9 are first values; the
    bots reset the ladder, the thresholds and the ceilings.
L8. Accepted: the round and the rate machines are grouped too; if the gate still misses, the
    engineer reports the largest remaining kind of event.
L9. Accepted: the yard plates (the baler first) stand in the south yard by the tipping floor; the
    back field holds the shredder line, the halls and the lead line. Ruling 7.1's "the back field
    with the first works plate" reads: the back field opens, and the first works plate stands by
    the tipping floor.
L10. Accepted: back-field plates are fed from bays and empty into bays through a belt weigher, with
    no hands. The hand's worth in stage 2 is the delivered lots, the crate, the yard sale, the
    trucks, commissioning and the first pours.
L11. THE SOUND PACK. First encode the music at a lower rate that a listener cannot tell apart; only
    if that does not make room does this game's limit rise to 3.4 MB.
L12. Accepted: a store-only reader of the country for this game; the four other games' region rule
    keeps its fallback (no shipped game changes).
L13. For the trade critic: the bridge's sign.
L14. Intended: the Settings switch for packs shows from the second session wherever the country
    allows packs, whatever the rung.
L15. THE TRADE BOOK MOVES TO STAGE 3 (it belongs with the dealers). Nothing of it is built in stage
    2: remove its state, tab and event from this document.
L16. Accepted: grouping from rung 3 only, so that the first hour stays as measured. When the first
    hour is next measured anyway, the bots try grouping from rung 1 and the lead decides.
L17. Accepted: the worked example names "a plate at §80,000" until the bots name the plate.
